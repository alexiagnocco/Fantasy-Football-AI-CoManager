What the champions' rosters say about ADP value

  • Rounds 1–3 buy floor, not upside. Winning first-three-pick combos were almost always two RBs + one WR taken at or near slot — no champion reached significantly above market early. The edge came later.
  • The title-winning value picks landed in rounds 5–8: Ja'Marr Chase R6 (2021), Amon-Ra St. Brown R5 (2022), Deebo Samuel R8 (2020), Malik Nabers R6 (2024), Isiah Pacheco R6 (2023). One rookie/breakout WR swing in that window shows up on nearly every title roster.
  • Elite TE early has worked twice: Kittle R3 (2021) and Andrews R3 (2022) both won it all. Otherwise champions took TE R6–8.
  • QB is a round 5–8 position here. The only early-QB title was Burrow at R4 in 2023. Everyone else waited — or streamed.
  • Drafts don't finish rosters. Champions retained 7–11 of 16 drafted players (median 9). The 2023 and 2025 champs kept just 7 — waiver churn is half the trophy.

Market behavior: trades & waivers

  • Trades are dead. 33 trades league-wide in ten seasons; the two most decorated franchises have made zero, ever. Do not build a draft plan that depends on trading out of a mistake.
  • Waivers are the real market. League-wide, roughly 3,000 adds over ten years. High-volume waiver managers (dmgbasketball12: 308 adds, Jbfishki: 299, CarterMull00: 287) out-churn you (192) by ~50%. Given champions rebuild ~44% of their roster in-season, this is likely a bigger gap than any draft-day decision.
  • FAAB (in place since 2022, $150 budget): spending is uniform — nearly everyone burns $110–150/yr. No one hoards, so early-season FAAB discipline can buy you the one uncontested week-10 league-winner.

The 2026 draft plan this history writes

  • Open RB-RB unless value screams otherwise. Every champion for eight straight years took an R1 RB; five of the last six opened RB-RB. In half-PPR with 2 RB + FLEX, this league's results say RB scarcity is real.
  • Rounds 5–8 are where titles are drafted. Reserve at least two picks in that window for high-variance breakout WRs — that archetype (Chase '21, ARSB '22, Nabers '24) appears on more title rosters than any early pick.
  • Wait on QB — but watch two managers. dafiscella50 (avg R3.7) and jdperdom (avg R4.0) take QBs early and can start a run. When the second QB goes, you still don't need to move; nine of ten champs got theirs R5+ or streamed.
  • TE: take an elite one at R3 if the board offers it, otherwise punt to R6–8. Both strategies have won here; the middle (R4–5 non-elite TE) appears on zero title rosters.
  • Kicker/D-ST last two rounds. No champion has ever spent earlier — this league punishes it via the waiver churn that follows.
  • Plan for the churn. Draft rounds 11–16 as lottery tickets you expect to drop, and budget your season around out-adding the 300-add managers. Half the roster that wins this league in December isn't drafted in September.
  • Seeding matters less than making the dance. Half the champions were 4-seeds or worse; 7-6 and 7-7 teams have won it all. The goal is entry, then peak in weeks 15–17.
